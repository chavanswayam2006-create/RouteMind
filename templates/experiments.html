Experiment 1 — PEAS & Formal Problem Formulation

Defines the AI agent using the standard Russell & Norvig PEAS taxonomy and generates formal state-space transitions from Start Node A to Goal Node G.

Performance Measure (P)

  • Minimize total transit duration (minutes).
  • Minimize route cost with congestion multipliers.
  • Zero traversal of blocked road corridors (hard constraint).
  • 100% on-time SLA fulfillment for high-priority packages.

Environment (E)

  • 7 interconnected city distribution hubs (A to G).
  • Distance (km), travel times (min), and dynamic congestion (0-100%).
  • Fully observable, deterministic transitions, sequential decision space.

Actuators (A)

  • Dispatch authorization commands.
  • Next-hop route selector (A → D → E → G).
  • Dynamic detour re-routing trigger upon incident detection.
  • ETA telemetry updates to destination docking bay.

Sensors (S)

  • Road obstruction / closure barrier indicators.
  • Live corridor congestion sensors (0-100%).
  • GPS hub-arrival geofence confirmations.
  • Estimated transit time telemetric sensors.
